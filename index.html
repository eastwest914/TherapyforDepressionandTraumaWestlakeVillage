About Depression And Trauma Therapy

We are a mental health practice in Westlake Village, California, centered on helping people live with and move beyond depression and the effects of trauma.

Our work draws on trauma-focused methods, including EMDR, alongside traditional talk therapy and whole-person support that looks at sleep, stress and daily routines.

Some people arrive after a single overwhelming event, others after years of carrying something quietly. Both are welcome, and neither needs to have everything figured out beforehand.

The office is open Monday through Friday from 9 am to 6 pm. Payment can be made by cash or credit card.

About Depression And Trauma Therapy

A Calm Place to Work Through Depression and Trauma

Depression And Trauma Therapy was created for people who want focused, compassionate care rather than a rushed appointment. Our practice in Westlake Village concentrates on depression, trauma-related conditions, PTSD and the anxiety and mood changes that often come with them.

We combine evidence-informed techniques with a warm, collaborative style. You set the direction, we offer guidance and tools, and together we adjust the plan as your needs change. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger or crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.
